## The difference

- **Up-front**: the plan pays eligible legal costs as the case proceeds (per its terms).
- **Reimbursement**: you may pay costs first and seek reimbursement later (per its terms).
- **Mixed**: some expenses may be paid up-front and others reimbursed.

## Questions to ask

1. **What triggers payment?** (arrest, charges filed, self-defense claim, etc.)
2. **Are there caps?** (per-incident, per-year, or category limits)
3. **How fast is reimbursement?** and what documentation is required?
