## Why it matters

Attorney choice affects who represents you and how quickly representation can begin.

## Common patterns

- **Yes**: you can choose your own attorney (often within program requirements).
- **Limited**: you can choose from a panel/network or must get approval.
- **No**: the program assigns counsel.

Always verify the details in the provider’s written terms and note any approvals, networks, or reimbursement rules.
