## Quick checklist

Use this checklist to shortlist 2–4 plans fast:

- **Payment style**: Is it up-front, reimbursement, or mixed?
- **Attorney choice**: Can you pick your own attorney, or is it limited?
- **Waiting period**: How long after signup before benefits apply?
- **Family coverage**: Are spouse/dependents included, or extra cost?
- **Coverage scope**: Criminal defense, civil defense/damages, or both?

## What to do next

- Start on the [comparison table](/compare).
- Open 2–4 plans in the compare view (use the “Compare” checkboxes).
- On each plan page, read **Exclusions & gotchas** and check **Sources + last verified**.
